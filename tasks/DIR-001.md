## Finding

Archguard's own architecture has a bidirectional dependency cycle: `src/types/config-global.ts`
imports from `src/analysis`, while `src/analysis` imports from `src/types`, creating a
`types ↔ analysis` loop. This was detected by archguard's own cycle detection tooling
but cannot be resolved autonomously — it's a structural defect in the codebase that
requires architectural refactoring.
